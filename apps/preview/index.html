VJS·1000 quick guide

  1. START to play. Press again: +30 sec.
  2. Type 1 · 3 · 0, then START: jumps to 1:30. Hold a number: jump to 10–90%.
  3. STOP pauses. Again: back to the start.
  4. DEFROST ½× · POPCORN 2× (listen…)
  5. Turn POWER for volume. SERVE = fullscreen. Open the door to pause.
  6. Flip the light switch on the wall. Watch the picture light the kitchen.
